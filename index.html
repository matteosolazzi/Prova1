What I do now

I lead the marketing & business development department — a team of fifteen people spread across marketing, communication, BD, and design, working from Italy, France, Spain, and Germany.

How I work

Strategy and execution, same head. I'm in every weekly review with the team, but I still write briefs and review designs. I don't believe in separating "those who think" from "those who do".

Numbers are the common language. CAC, ROAS, retention, churn, growth rate. If it doesn't move one of these, it doesn't ship. If it does ship, it gets measured.

Cross-functional by default. Marketing, BD, design, and product are the same team chasing the same goal. Silos are a problem to solve, not a structure to respect.

Built to scale. When I joined Daze we were ten. Today we're eighty. I've spent the last five years building processes and teams that survive the jump from "everyone does everything" to "everyone does one thing well".

What I'm thinking about

I spend a lot of time thinking about how early-stage teams scale go-to-market in capital-intensive industries — energy, hardware, climate-tech. It's a space where standard SaaS marketing playbooks break, and where I've spent the last five years figuring things out in practice.

If you're building something in this space, drop me a line. Happy to chat.
